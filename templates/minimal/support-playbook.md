# Support playbook

## Categories

- `bug`: The product behaves incorrectly, throws an error, loses data, or blocks
  a documented workflow.
- `feature`: The customer asks for new behavior or a product capability that
  does not exist yet.
- `question`: The customer needs explanation, configuration guidance, or policy
  clarification.

## Priority

- `urgent`: Revenue, renewal, security, or multiple users are blocked today.
- `normal`: A workflow is degraded but a workaround exists.
- `low`: Informational, cosmetic, or no customer workflow is blocked.

## Routing

- Bugs with urgent priority go to engineering after support writes clear
  reproduction steps.
- Feature requests go to product with one user story and acceptance criteria.
- Questions stay with support unless the playbook lacks enough information.
